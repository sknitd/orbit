Questions? Answers.

What is a Mac notch app?

A Mac notch app is a macOS utility that turns the camera notch on your MacBook display into a live workspace. Instead of dead space, it shows tools — now playing, clipboard history, weather, AI chat, system stats — in an interactive panel. OmniNotch opens on hover by default, or on click if you prefer (Settings → Open Notch). It ships twenty tools, including on-device Ask Omni AI, timers, stocks, and a file shelf.

Does OmniNotch open on hover or click?

Both are supported. By default the notch opens when you hover the strip. In Settings → Open Notch you can switch to On Click so it stays closed until you click. Hover Delay still applies when you use hover open.

Which Macs does OmniNotch support?

OmniNotch runs on macOS Sonoma 14 and later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. On MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models with a physical notch (M1 through M4), the panel lives inside the notch. On notch-less Macs — including older MacBook Pros and Mac desktops — OmniNotch draws its own floating notch at the top of the screen.

Why does macOS say OmniNotch can’t be opened?

This build isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Notarization needs an active Apple Developer account, and we’re renewing ours with Apple support right now. Until then, macOS shows a warning the first time you open OmniNotch. On macOS 15 Sequoia and later: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier: in Finder, right‑click OmniNotch → Open → click Open. You only do this once. To check your download is the real one, compare its SHA‑256 checksum with the one on the setup page.

My Mac doesn't have a notch. Can I still use it?

Yes. On notch-less Macs OmniNotch draws its own floating notch at the top of your screen, so every feature still works.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment for a lifetime license, including all future updates. No account, no recurring bill. OmniNotch is $5 until October 5, then $19.99. Checkout may show your local currency; display prices can vary by location.

I already own OmniNotch. Do I pay for 2.0?

No. Every license includes lifetime updates, so OmniNotch 2.0 is free for everyone who has already bought it. Download it again from omninotch.app/download.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Clipboard history and dropped files stay on your Mac. There's no cloud and no telemetry — it even skips password-manager and transient clips. Ask Omni runs on-device via Apple Intelligence Foundation Models, so prompts never leave your Mac.

What is Ask Omni?

Private on-device chat in the notch, powered by Apple Intelligence Foundation Models. Rewrite, extract, summarize, and brainstorm without sending anything to a remote server. It requires a Mac with Apple Intelligence enabled.

How does activation work?

After you buy, you're taken to a setup page with the Mac download, and your license key is also emailed. Enter the key once in OmniNotch — it activates on up to 3 Macs, is stored in your macOS Keychain, and keeps working offline.
